# Picktopia Richmond Hill Memberships

Picktopia Richmond Hill offers three membership options for players who want priority booking, preferred access, and better value on court time, open play, leagues, clinics, and club programming.

All memberships require a 12-month term commitment. A first-month deposit plus HST is collected at registration. Launch pricing applies for the initial 12-month term.

Full-Access Membership
$139.99/month launch rate
Regular rate: $169.99/month

Best for dedicated players who want access across peak and off-peak hours.

Includes:

- Peak and off-peak court access
- 14-day advance booking window
- Member discounts on tournaments, leagues, clinics, and programs
- Up to 4 consecutive bookings at a time
- First-month deposit: $139.99 + HST

Core-Access Membership
$99.99/month launch rate
Regular rate: $119.99/month

Best for regular players looking for strong value, especially during non-peak hours.

Includes:

- Free non-peak hours
- 50% off peak court fees and program fees
- 14-day advance booking window
- Member privilege access to club programs and events
- First-month deposit: $99.99 + HST

General-Access Annual
$499.99/year launch rate
Regular rate: $699.99/year

Best for players who want basic member privileges without a monthly membership.

Includes:

- 50% off court fees and program fees
- 14-day advance booking window
- Member privilege access to leagues and events
- Annual membership term
- Initial deposit + HST collected at registration

Current Non-Member Fees

- Court booking: $55 + HST
- Open play: $22 + HST per player
- Taxes apply at checkout
- Spaces are subject to availability
